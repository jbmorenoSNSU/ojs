Journal Publications Unit

The Surigao del Norte State University Journal Publications Unit manages ethical, peer-reviewed scholarly publications and showcases the publication endeavors of faculty, staff, and students, strengthening research culture and enhancing the university's academic visibility overall.

The Journal Publications Unit (JPU) oversees the publication of Surigao del Norte State University's (SNSU) international peer-reviewed journals. Operating under the Office of the University Research Director (OURD), the JPU manages the entire publication process, from Call for Publication to the online posting of journal articles/papers submitted by faculty, students, and staff both within and outside the university. All journals are hosted on SNSU Journal Publications Unit Open Journal System, part of the university's website. The SNSU Journal Publications Unit serves as the official scholarly online publication of the university. It provides a platform for the dissemination of original research, reviews, and guidelines translating the processes and publication operations to ensure transparency, quality, and ethical compliance.

Our Aims

The Journal Publications Unit is dedicated to disseminating high-quality, peer-reviewed research that advances knowledge, fosters innovation, and promotes academic and professional excellence within and across disciplines.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To be a globally recognized academic journal publisher that upholds excellence, innovation, and integrity.

Mission

To publish high-quality, peer-reviewed research that contributes to academic discourse, community development, and international contexts.

Objectives

01

Provide a reputable platform for scholars to publish original and impactful research.

02

Uphold rigorous peer review and ethical publishing standards.

03

Promote interdisciplinary collaboration and knowledge sharing.

04

Support the research, extension, and innovation agenda of SNSU.
